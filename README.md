# Hi, I'm Shubham Vishwakarma 👋

### Data Analyst | Design Engineer | Supervisor | Educator

My path to becoming a **Data and Business Analyst** hasn’t been a straight line, and I think that’s my biggest strength.I started out in engineering, earning my **Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering from R.G.P.V Bhopal**, followed by a year of intensive **Pre-Sea Training in Marine Engineering at IMS Goa**.Before diving into the world of data, I spent a few years gaining real-world experience across very different industries:

### 🔹 On the Floor

Spending a year as a **Floor Supervisor** taught me how to manage operations under pressure and solve logistical problems on the fly.

### 🔹 At the Drawing Board

Working as a **Design Engineer** for a year sharpened my analytical thinking and taught me how to break down complex, technical systems.

### 🔹 In the Classroom

Spending two years as an **Educator** forced me to master the art of communication—specifically, how to take complicated ideas and explain them clearly to anyone.

Every single one of these roles taught me something valuable, but more than anything, they taught me how to look at a chaotic situation, find the underlying pattern, and make things run better.That curiosity eventually led me to data.To bridge my engineering roots with my passion for data-driven strategy, I completed a **Post Graduate Certificate in Data Science by the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC)** and transitioned into **Data and Business Analysis**.Today, I combine my technical foundation, real-world operational experience, and analytical skills to transform raw data into clear, actionable business strategies.When I’m not digging into data models or building dashboards, I’m usually looking for the next interesting problem to solve.

# 📊 My Approach to Data: The Analytics Maturity Model

## Phase 1: Descriptive Analytics (What Happened?)

I treat data cleaning and ETL as foundational engineering steps.

Using **SQL, Power Query, and Python**, I transform messy datasets into clean, single sources of truth, then build intuitive **Power BI dashboards** so stakeholders have instant visibility into operational performance.

---

## Phase 2: Diagnostic Analytics (Why Did It Happen?)

### Process Optimization

Drawing on my background as a **Floor Supervisor** and **Design Engineer**, I don't just look at chart anomalies—I conduct **root-cause analysis**.

I translate business requirements into efficient data-driven workflows, pinpointing exactly where operational bottlenecks and inefficiencies live.

---

## Phase 3: Predictive Analytics (What Will Happen Next?)

### Predictive & Analytical Models

I scale beyond historical reporting by leveraging **Python, statistical analysis, and Machine Learning**.

By building forecasting models (such as cost predictions or trend analysis), I help organizations shift from reactive troubleshooting to proactive strategy.

---

## Phase 4: Prescriptive Analytics (How Can We Make It Happen?)

### Strategic Decision Support

The ultimate goal of my data pipeline.

I deliver actionable insights and automated reporting structures that guide stakeholders on the next best operational step, directly linking data analysis to measurable business growth.
